Fishmond

An underwater logic game for kids. Guide the little fish to its food: slide the octopuses and jellyfish out of the way and collect the treasure!

  • 180 levels that get harder step by step, the first 15 are free.
  • One-time purchase, no subscription.
  • No ads, no tracking, no data collection.
  • Plays offline, in English and Hungarian.

Available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store.
